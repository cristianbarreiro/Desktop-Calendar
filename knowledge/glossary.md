# Domain & Project Glossary

## Core Terms

- **Widget**: The ultra-compact, desktop-docked window providing quick glanceable calendar and day details.
- **Full Application**: The primary management window containing calendar event management, notes, and preferences.
- **Event**: A time-bounded or all-day calendar item associated with a specific date and time span.
- **Note**: An offline text entry for thoughts, checklists, or scratchpad content.
- **Day Cell**: An individual day box within the 7x6 month grid.
- **Event Indicator**: A visual dot or marker on a day cell indicating the presence of scheduled events.
- **Tray Expansion**: The dynamic vertical expansion of the widget window when a specific day is clicked to show its events.
- **Composition Root**: The startup layer (`CalendarWidget.App`) where DI registrations, configuration, and window lifecycles are initialized.
